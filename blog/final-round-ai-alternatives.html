What to look for in an interview copilot

  • Speed. A suggestion that arrives after you've started talking is useless. Look for help that starts in about a second.
  • Your own experience. Generic textbook answers sound generic. The tool should use your resume and the answers you prepared, with your project names and numbers.
  • Misheard questions. Live speech-to-text mishears technical terms. A good copilot still understands what was asked.
  • Price for how you actually use it. Most people need help for a few interviews, not every day of the year.

2. LockedIn AI

A subscription interview copilot that roundups describe as aimed at delivery and confidence under pressure. Its stealth features cost extra: about $55 a month for the general plan and about $120 a month with stealth, or a lifetime option.

3. Cluely

Cluely started as an interview tool and now positions itself as a general AI meeting assistant. The Pro plan is $19.99 a month, but screen-share invisibility is in the $149.99-a-month tier.

4. Sensei AI

Offers a free tier and real-time interview help; paid plans are listed at about $89 a month, or about $24 a month when paid yearly.
