How it works during a live interview

  1. It hears the question. TranzyNote listens to your computer's audio, so it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex or any other call app. Nothing joins the meeting.
  2. It understands what was asked, even when speech-to-text mishears a technical term.
  3. It shows a short answer you can say in your own words: one clear opening line and a few key points.
  4. It uses your experience. If you uploaded notes that already answer the question, the suggestion uses your project, your steps and your object names instead of a textbook answer.
  5. It remembers the conversation, including what you told it about yourself at the start, so follow-up answers stay consistent.

Set it up in 10 minutes

  1. Download TranzyNote from the home page and sign in with Google or email.
  2. Open the interview type you want (or make your own) and upload your resume and preparation notes. Word, PDF, Excel, PowerPoint and text files all work.
  3. Type the role and company before you start, for example "SAP BW consultant at Capgemini".
  4. Start a practice session using your free minutes and ask yourself a few common questions to see the answers.

Tips for better answers

  • Write your best stories as notes. For each big project: the problem, what you did (with real object, table or tool names) and the result. The assistant will reuse them.
  • Keep a list of the questions you were asked before, with your answers. Repeat rounds at service companies often ask similar questions.
  • Say it in your own words. Use the suggestion as a reminder of the points, not a script to read.
